# hooks.md — hook patterns

Shared by `ideias-de-post`, `post-instagram` and `post-linkedin`. The hook is most of a post's value:
pick the patterns that fit the topic and her real material. Examples are in her register; never reuse
them verbatim, and never put a number or a story in a hook that is not true and on file.

| # | Pattern | Shape | Fits | Example (style only) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Reframe | a common belief, flipped in one line | her core message (from `negocio.md`) | "<Crença comum> não é <X>. É <Y>." |
| 2 | Pergunta específica | a question the reader answers in her head | an everyday moment her audience lives | "Você já <situação concreta> e pensou: <frase da cliente>?" |
| 3 | História vulnerável | a real moment of hers, told plainly | the stories in her `escrita.md` | "Eu <vivi algo difícil>. E demorei pra admitir." |
| 4 | Nome do padrão | name the pattern the reader lives | the patterns her files name | "Isso tem nome: <padrão>." |
| 5 | Conceito com credencial | a researcher or author, one-line credential, one idea | the references in her `escrita.md`, with the source named | "<Autor>, <credencial em uma linha>, explica que <ideia>." |
| 6 | Lista de dores | 2-3 pains she recognizes, in the reader's words | the pains in `negocio.md`, verbatim when possible | "<Dor 1>. <Dor 2>. E a sensação de <dor 3>." |
| 7 | Bastidores | what nobody tells about her field | her other network, insider view | "No <meio dela>, a gente raramente fala sobre isso." |
| 8 | Contraste | two kinds of people or choices | value contrasts | "Tem quem <escolha A>. E tem quem <escolha B>." |
| 9 | Recibo | something she tested or lived, with a real result | only with a real fact on file | "Fiz X por 30 dias. O que mudou foi Y." |

## Picking

| Topic shape | Best patterns |
|---|---|
| A concept or method (her method, a named idea) | 4, 5, 1 |
| Her own story | 3, 9 |
| The reader's pain | 6, 2 |
| A strong opinion | 1, 8 |
| Her field from the inside (LinkedIn) | 7, 5, 1 |

## The first-words test

Read the first 3-5 words of each hook. Do they create curiosity, recognition or emotion — or do they
sound like throat-clearing ("Hoje eu quero", "No mundo de hoje", "A verdade é que")? If a hook fails,
regenerate it with a different pattern before showing it.
