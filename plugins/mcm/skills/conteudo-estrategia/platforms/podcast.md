# Podcast

Her show, audio first, on the host she picks. Not a social platform, but it follows the same switch: on only when the platforms line in her strategy names it. An advanced format: the strategy talks it through longer than the others (conteudo-estrategia, "The podcast").

## When to recommend it
When she likes to talk more than write, her expertise is what she sells, and she sells something people decide on over time (coaching, a program, consulting). Only when she can keep a season going: about one episode every two weeks for eight episodes. Not as a first step for someone who isn't posting yet: the posts are what bring listeners.

## Account
Her host. **Spotify for Creators** by default: free, the most common, and it sends the show to Spotify; it also gives her an RSS feed for Apple Podcasts and the rest. Any host works the same way for us: she pastes the kit. What we keep, in `departamentos/conteudo/podcast.md`: show name, host, show link, the feed address when her host shows it, show art file, the current season and the last episode number, YouTube connected yes/no.

## Formats
From the strategy library's podcast catalog: one-idea solo (default), her audience's question, story and lesson, client case, myth or hot take, interview (later), trailer, season opener and finale.

## Caption
The episode's own text, the kit, from the playbook's podcast section:
1. **Title:** 3 options, up to 150 characters (shorter reads better), what someone would search for or want to hear. Never the episode number in the title: it has its own field.
2. **Description:** plain text, no HTML. The first two lines carry it (they're all most apps show): who it's for and what they get. Then 3-5 lines on what's inside, the one ask with its link, and the chapters.
3. **Chapters:** at the end of the description, one per line, `(00:00) Title`: the first at 00:00, at least 3, in order, titles under 40 characters, no numbers in front (Spotify reads them for shows hosted anywhere; other apps show them as timestamps).
4. **Fields:** season and episode number, episode type (full, trailer or bonus), explicit yes/no, episode art only when it differs from the show art.
5. **Transcript:** an `.srt` file from the episode's words, for hosts that take one.

## Scheduling
Nothing is scheduled from here in v1: she publishes in her host with Claude's numbered steps and the kit in order (conteudo-podcast). Her host's own schedule is fine: "Schedule" instead of "Now" in Spotify for Creators. **Audio to spec** before it goes up: about -16 LUFS, true peak no higher than -1 dB, MP3 at 128 kbps stereo, 44.1 kHz. **YouTube**, later and optional: YouTube can pull each new episode from her feed as a video over her show art, in some countries only; a one-time step in YouTube Studio, offered once she has a season going.

## Results
Light in v1. Episode plays and followers stay in her host. The monthly review reads the podcast through the posts cut from each episode, by cycle, and through replies and calls she mentions. Judge a show by the season, not the episode: podcasts grow slowly in the apps, and most new listeners find a show through YouTube and social.
