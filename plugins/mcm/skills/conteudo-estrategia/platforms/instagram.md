# Instagram

## When to recommend it
Always: it's the default platform and where people check a business out. On unless she says no.

## Account
Her business Instagram account (a professional account), connected in MCM's Social Planner (setup step 12, Guia D).

## Formats
- **Carousel, illustrated carousel, text post:** the slides as PNG images, 1080x1440, in slide order.
- **Photo post, photo carousel:** her photos as JPEG, 1080x1440, in order (up to 10).
- **Reel:** one vertical MP4 (1080x1920), shown on the feed.

## Caption
The main caption: the playbook's caption formula as written (hook line, plain explanation, the wedge, the CTA for the layer, 3-5 hashtags). "Link na bio" is fine here. Hashtags in Portuguese.

## Scheduling
In the shared `create-post` with the other platforms that use the main caption: its account ID from `ghl.md` in `accountIds`; `instagramPostDetails: {type: post}` for images, `{type: reel, showOnFeed: true}` for a reel.

## Results
Saves and shares say more than likes; comments next. Reach shows how far the hook travelled.
