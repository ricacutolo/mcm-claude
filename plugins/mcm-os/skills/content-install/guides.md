# Canva guides for the content setup

Three short guides, handed to her at the Canva steps of the content setup. Written so someone who has never used Canva can follow along. Claude gives them in plain words, one step at a time, filling in her own colors, fonts and logo files from `about-me/brand.md` or `departments/content/brand-style.md`.

- **Guide 0** — first time in Canva (setup step 5)
- **Guide A** — Brand Kit colors (setup step 7)
- **Guide B** — logo, fonts and hand fixes on a template (setup step 8, and any hand fix after a template or post design)

---

## Guide 0: First time in Canva

1. Go to canva.com and sign up with the email you want your designs under. The free plan is enough to start.
2. Canva asks "What will you be using Canva for?" Pick **Work and business**.
3. If the next screens offer a team setup or a free Pro trial, skip them. A trial starts billing when it ends unless you cancel. The screens after this vary; skip anything that asks for payment details.

## Guide A: Set up your Brand Kit (about 5 minutes)

Your Brand Kit is where Canva keeps your colors, so every design uses the same ones.

1. Go to canva.com and log in.
2. On the home page, look at the menu on the left and click **Brand**.
3. If a Brand Kit is already there, click it to open it. If not, click the dropdown next to Brand Kit, choose **Create personal Brand Kit**, name it after your business and confirm.
4. **Colors.** Open the Colors section and add your 3 brand colors by pasting each color code: your dark color, your light color and your accent color (Claude gives you the codes). The free plan allows 3 colors, so we use your 3 main ones.
5. **Fonts.** On the free plan you can't set fonts in the Brand Kit (it asks for Pro or Business). Skip it. Fonts get set on the template itself (Guide B), and every copy keeps them.
6. **Logo.** Skip it here. On the free plan, logos can't go in the Brand Kit, so we use Uploads instead (Guide B).

If a color or font doesn't show up the way you expect, stop and tell Claude. Don't swap it for a random one.

## Guide B: Put your brand on a template (about 10 minutes)

1. Open the design from the link Claude gives you.
2. **Fonts** (only if Claude says a font needs fixing). Click a heading, hold Shift and click the other headings on that slide to select them together. Click the font name on the top toolbar, search for your heading font (Claude tells you which) and click it. Do the same for the body text with your body font. Repeat for each slide. If a font doesn't come up in the search, stop and tell Claude.
3. **Upload your logo.** Uploads only appears inside a design, not on the home page. In the narrow menu on the far left of the editor, click **Uploads** (if it isn't there, check **Apps** or **More** at the bottom of that menu), then **Upload files**, and choose your logo file. Upload two versions: a light one (white) for dark slides and a color one for light slides. Claude tells you which files to use.
4. **Place the logo.** Go to the slide Claude names (usually the last one). Click your uploaded logo and it drops onto the slide. Drag it into place and pull a corner to resize it.
5. **Remove the placeholder.** If there's leftover placeholder text or a placeholder logo where yours now goes, click it and press Delete.
6. Done. Canva saves on its own. Tell Claude it's ready.

Sources (Canva help): [Brand Kit limits on Free](https://www.canva.com/help/color-palettes/), [personal Brand Kit](https://www.canva.com/help/personal-brand-kit/), [brand fonts](https://www.canva.com/help/brand-fonts/), [brand logos are paid-only](https://www.canva.com/help/brand-logos/), [apply text styles from the Edit panel](https://www.canva.com/help/apply-brand-kit-text-styles/) (says Free, but setting the styles needs a paid plan). Canva moves buttons now and then; if a click isn't where the guide says, describe what you see and Claude finds it with you.
