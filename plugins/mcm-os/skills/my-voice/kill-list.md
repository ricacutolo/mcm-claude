# kill-list.md — signs of AI-written text (US English)

The floor every drafting skill in this plugin scans against before showing a draft. Her own
`about-me/writing.md` adds personal rules on top; her voice always wins, this list is the minimum.

How to use: after drafting, search the draft for each item below. If one is found, rewrite that
sentence (not the whole text) and scan again until clean.

## Banned openers

- "In today's world…" / "In this day and age…" / "In an increasingly…world"
- "Have you ever stopped to think…" when it is a generic question (a specific question in her style is fine)
- "Let's talk about…" / "Today I want to talk about…" / "In this post…"
- "The truth is…" / "Let me tell you…" / "Picture this…"
- "Great question!" / "Absolutely!" / "Of course!"

## AI cliché phrases

- "Let's go on this journey together" / "your journey" as a generic abstraction
- "It's not about X, it's about Y" (one use at most, and only if it is genuinely her point)
- "Now more than ever" / "At the end of the day" / "When all is said and done"
- "Makes all the difference" / "A real game-changer" / "Flip the switch" as filler
- "Without further ado" / "Let's dive in!" used as a template close
- "I hope this helps!" / "Feel free to reach out with any questions" as an automatic ending

## Filler words

- unlock, elevate, leverage, supercharge, empower, reimagine — as filler, not as a real claim
- "amazing", "powerful", "transformative", "revolutionary" stacked as hype
- "simply", "literally", "basically", "really" as padding

## Structure tics

- Em dash (—) anywhere in a post or message. Zero. She pauses with ellipses (…), commas or a new line.
- Lists of exactly three adjectives or three items when the content is not actually a list (the same three abstract nouns every time)
- "First… Second… Third…" when the items are not real steps (her own step sequences are fine)
- Every paragraph the same length; every sentence ending in a punchline
- Rhetorical question followed immediately by its own answer, repeated more than once
- Emoji at the start of every line, or more than 1-2 emoji in a post

## Tone tics

- Flattering the reader in the first line ("To every amazing woman reading this…")
- Over-qualifying ("In my humble opinion…")
- Formal register she never uses: "Dear Sir or Madam", "per our conversation", "furthermore", "henceforth"
- Hard sell, scarcity or urgency she would not use ("Last spots left!", "Hurry!")
- Claims, numbers or results she cannot back

## Never

- Invent a client story, a testimonial, a quote or a number. If the draft needs proof and none is on file, leave a clearly marked placeholder: `[REAL TESTIMONIAL HERE]`.
