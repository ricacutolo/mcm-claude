# Substack

Her Substack publication: **articles** (the newsletter, also on Substack) and **Notes** (Substack's short social feed, where people find her). Same switch as the other platforms: on only when the platforms line in her strategy names it. Kept light on purpose: it reuses the newsletter and the posts, it doesn't add a new writing load.

## When to recommend it
When the newsletter is on and she writes ideas people want to read more of (teachers, coaches, consultants, anyone whose expertise is the product), or when she already has a Substack. Not as a first platform, and not instead of her list in MCM: MCM stays her main list, because her leads and clients live there.

## Account
Her own Substack publication. There's no connection for it in Claude: the system works it through the browser inside the Claude app, where she signs in to Substack once (setup step 13). Her publication address lives in `ghl.md` under **Substack**.

## Formats
- **Article:** every newsletter issue, published on Substack too, on the same day and time as the email. Substack emails it to her Substack subscribers. Someone on both lists gets it twice; that's accepted.
- **Note:** 2-3 a week, cut from what she's already making: an angle from the current newsletter cycle, or the core line of a recent post. Never written from scratch, never a new topic. Notes don't count toward one post a day.
- Nothing else for now (no podcast, video or chat).

## Caption
**Article:** the approved newsletter, as is. Title = the subject she picked; subtitle = the preview text; drop the "Hi <first name>," line (Substack has no first names). 1-3 tags from the issue's topic, in her words, reused from issue to issue so they stay few.

**Note:**
1. The first one or two sentences are the hook: a claim, a number, a pain she names, a belief she challenges.
2. Short: about 30-120 words, like a message to a friend. White space, no hashtags.
3. One aim per Note: one idea, at most one question.
4. No hard selling. A link to the latest article is fine now and then; most Notes carry none.

## Scheduling
Through the browser, by the Substack steps (content-substack). Articles: scheduled with "Schedule time to email and publish", audience Everyone, in her time zone, after her yes at the newsletter's schedule gate. Notes: Substack's own Note scheduler, after her yes. Never "Send to everyone now" or "Post". Default Note days: two or three weekdays apart from each other, at her post time.

## Results
New Substack subscribers per month first, then each Note's replies and restacks, likes least. Read in her Substack dashboard through the browser at the monthly review. Judge it over two to three months; if it brings no subscribers, recommend switching it off.
