# LinkedIn

## When to recommend it
When the people she sells to are professionals, business owners or companies (coaches, consultants, trainers, B2B services, anyone selling to people at work). Not for most local consumer services. Same days as Instagram is fine; LinkedIn is read on workdays, so recommend her slots fall Monday to Friday.

## Account
Her **personal profile**, not a company page: profiles get far more reach on LinkedIn, and people follow people there. Connected in MCM's Social Planner (setup step 13, Guide D). It must be her real profile.

## Formats
- **Carousel, illustrated carousel:** the same slides, sent as one **document** (PDF) post, the swipeable kind LinkedIn shows in the feed. The document gets a short title: the hook in plain words.
- **Text post:** works well on LinkedIn as plain text, without the image, when the caption carries the whole idea. Default: send the image like the other platforms; offer the text-only version when the post is mostly words.
- **Photo post, photo carousel:** the same JPEGs as images (not a document), in order. **Headline photo:** the same PNG as one image. A real photo of her or her work does well here.
- **Reel:** off on LinkedIn for now. Vertical video looks wrong on a desktop feed and video reach there is low. Reels go only to the other platforms.

## Caption
LinkedIn gets **its own caption**, written from the same idea and approved at the same copy gate as the main one:
1. The first two lines are the hook: they show before "see more", so they must make the click necessary. A different line from slide 1.
2. Lead with the lesson for people at work: what this changes in their day, their team, their results. Professional, plain, still her voice; never corporate.
3. Short paragraphs, one idea each, white space between. Longer than Instagram: about 120-250 words.
4. Ends with a question people can answer from their own experience, then the slot's ending. Comments matter most here, so the question is real, not "thoughts?".
5. The ask, LinkedIn version, on `ask` slots: credibility → "Want help with this? Send me a message." Never "link in bio". No links to other sites in the post; if she wants one, she adds it later as a comment herself.
6. 1-3 hashtags at the end, specific to the topic. Never more.

## Scheduling
Its **own `create-post`** (its caption differs): the LinkedIn account ID from `ghl.md` alone in `accountIds`, the LinkedIn caption as `summary`, the same permanent image URLs as `media`. Photo formats go as images, never as a document. For carousels: if `create-post` offers a LinkedIn document/PDF option, use it with the slides in order and the document title; if it doesn't, send the slides as images and note it in the post's run notes. Idempotency key: `<location ID>-post-NN-<slug>-linkedin`. Same slot as the other platforms unless the strategy sets a LinkedIn time.

## Results
Comments count most, then reposts with a comment, then saves; reactions least. Read impressions as reach. A new profile starts slow: judge LinkedIn over a month, and say so.
