# Facebook

## When to recommend it
When she has a Facebook Page, or her customers are on Facebook (local services, older audiences). It costs nothing extra: same post, same caption.

## Account
Her business Facebook Page (not her personal profile), connected in MCM's Social Planner (setup step 12, Guide D).

## Formats
- **Carousel, illustrated carousel, text post:** the same PNG images as Instagram, in slide order.
- **Photo post, photo carousel:** the same JPEGs as Instagram, in order.
- **Reel:** the same MP4 as Instagram.

## Caption
Shares the main caption with Instagram. No changes.

## Scheduling
In the shared `create-post` with Instagram: its account ID from `ghl.md` in `accountIds`; `facebookPostDetails: {type: post}` for images, `{type: reel}` for a reel.

## Results
Comments and shares say more than likes. Facebook reach is usually small for a new Page; judge it over a month, not a post.
