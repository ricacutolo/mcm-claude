# Strategy library

MCM's know-how: what works and what doesn't in content, by type of business. It ships with the plugin and is **read-only**: no skill ever edits this file. It changes only when MCM releases a new plugin version.

Each business keeps its own additions in `departments/content/library-notes.md` (written by content-review, on her yes): entries learned from her own results, and any post type or format added to her catalogs. Skills read this file first, then her notes; where they disagree, her notes win for her business.

## How an entry works
- One entry per thing tried or learned.
- Status: **unproven** (from research or a single post), **working** (held up across several posts or businesses), **dropped** (tried, didn't work).
- Evidence: where it comes from — the performance log or a named source.
- Nothing becomes **working** without real numbers behind it.

## Formats (catalog)
What a post looks like. Each business switches on the formats that fit it (face, photos, time). A template is format × post type.

| Format | What it is | Face | Needs | Fits post types |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Text post | One slide, one strong line | no | template | Quick win, Myth vs truth, Story, Question you always get, What you get |
| Carousel | Text slides | no | template | Leak, 3 mistakes, Quick win, How it works, Myth vs truth, Old way vs new way, What you get |
| Photo carousel | Slides with real photos | optional | her photos | Before and after, Behind the scenes, Who we are |
| Photo post | One photo + caption: her own, or one that speaks to her audience | optional | a photo | Who we are, Behind the scenes, Story, Leak |
| Illustrated carousel | Simple drawings or diagrams that explain how something works | no | template + Canva elements | How it works, Old way vs new way, Before and after, Leak |
| Animated diagram | An illustrated diagram in motion | no | phase 2 (motion graphics) | How it works, Old way vs new way |
| Screenshot post | A real text thread, calendar or review, with permission | no | the real thing | Proof |
| Talking-head reel | To camera, 30-60 s | yes | content-video (beta), face on camera | Who we are, Story, Question you always get |
| Voiceover reel | Voice over footage or screens | voice | phase 2 | How it works, Behind the scenes |
| Text-on-video reel | Captions over footage | no | phase 2 | Quick win, Myth vs truth, Leak |
| Stories | Casual, 24 hours | optional | later | any |
