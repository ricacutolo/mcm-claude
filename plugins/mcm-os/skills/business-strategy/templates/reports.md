# Reports to the CEO

_Written by the departments when they see something about the business, not their own work. Read and settled in the strategy session. A department only adds lines under Open; only /business-strategy moves them to Settled._

## Open
- YYYY-MM-DD · <department> · <what it saw, in one line> · (from: <which run, e.g. content review 2026-10-01 to 2026-10-31>)

## Settled
- YYYY-MM-DD · <department> · <the line> → <where it went: the strategy, its Open questions, or "no change"> (settled YYYY-MM-DD)
